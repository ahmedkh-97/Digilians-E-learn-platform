Ranked identity rule

Any activity that can write to or display a leaderboard requires a saved student name. Study-only browsing remains available without a name.

Junior section IDs

Each source bank file is treated as a stable official section. Section leaderboard exam IDs follow: official-junior-data-analysis-<trackId>-section-<NN>

The best attempt per player_id is used by the online leaderboard.

Section completion

A ranked section uses every official question in that section in original order. After submission the standard result screen shows:

  • Correct
  • Wrong
  • Unanswered
  • Accuracy
  • Time
  • Personal best
  • Online rank
  • Retake
  • Review answers
  • View ranking
  • Next section / Back to track

Professional isolation

Professional Data Analysis will be imported later as its own level with its own tracks, sections, progress and rankings. It must not share question IDs or leaderboard exam IDs with Junior.

Source integrity

Official question wording, options and official answers remain source-locked. Arabic explanations are platform-generated educational additions and remain labeled separately.
