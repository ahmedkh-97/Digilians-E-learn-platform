# Excel Beginner Learning Standard V3

## Goal

A learner with **zero prior Excel knowledge** should be able to enter a lesson, read it in order, and understand the concept without needing the original presentation open beside the platform.

## Learner-facing language

Do not teach with phrases such as:

- “المصدر بيشرح...”
- “المصدر بيقول...”
- “حسب المصدر...”

Those phrases describe the document, not the concept.

The lesson should explain the concept directly.

`Source Trace` remains at the bottom only for traceability. `SOURCE GAP`, `SOURCE INCONSISTENCY`, and `PRESENTATION CORRECTION` remain visible when needed.

## Beginner lesson order

1. **Understand the Idea — نفهمها من الصفر**
2. **Why Do I Need This? — هتستخدمها ليه؟**
3. **Concepts Made Simple — نفهم المصطلحات واحدة واحدة**
4. **Simple Example**
5. Existing visual / formula / steps where supported
6. Key Takeaways
7. Source Trace

## Example policy

Simple teaching examples may be added only when they explain a concept already supported by the uploaded material. They must be labeled:

`PLATFORM CLARIFICATION — مثال تعليمي لتبسيط مفهوم موجود في المادة`

## Readability

Beginner explanation is full-width. Do not squeeze the main Arabic teaching text into two narrow cards. Concept cards may use a 2-column layout on desktop and must collapse to one column on mobile.
