# V0.20.38 — Refactor Foundation QA

## Intent
Behavior-preserving architecture refactor foundation only.

## Production changes
- Extract existing `:root` and `[data-theme="dark"]` semantic tokens from `assets/css/style.css` into `assets/css/tokens.css` with exact values preserved.
- Load `tokens.css` before `style.css`.
- Route ranking preference persistence through `assets/js/storage.js` safe wrappers while preserving all legacy key names.
- Require `tokens.css` in Quick Local Check and Full Pre-Deploy.

## Explicitly unchanged
- Voucher PL-300 reviewed bank and visuals.
- Official QBank.
- Course Question Banks and Exams.
- Ranking scopes, activity IDs, ordering and online sync.
- Exam scoring, feedback, timer, resume and navigator behavior.
- My Mistakes and learner progress payloads.
